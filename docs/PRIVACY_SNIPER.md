# Zero Hour Sniper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Zero Hour Sniper ("the game") is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no chat and no online play.

## What the game itself stores

Your progress (contracts completed, money, rifles, settings) is saved in a file on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it.

## Advertising

The game shows advertisements through Google AdMob. To do this, Google's advertising software inside the game may collect and use:

- the advertising ID of your device;
- your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
- technical information about the device and how the advertisements were viewed.

Google uses this information to choose and measure advertisements. How Google handles it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off personalised advertising, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

We do not receive, store or sell any of this information ourselves.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the developer through the game's page on Google Play.
